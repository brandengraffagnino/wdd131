Pickleball Hub Site Plan

Site Name

Pickleball Hub

This name was chosen because it represents a central resource where visitors can learn about pickleball, equipment, rules, strategies, and places to play.

Site Purpose

The purpose of this website is to provide information about pickleball, including its history, rules, equipment, scoring, and beginner tips. The site will also help visitors find resources to improve their game.

Scenarios

  • What equipment do I need to begin playing pickleball?
  • How does scoring work in pickleball?
  • What are some basic strategies for beginners?
  • Where can I find places to play pickleball?

Color Scheme

Used for headings, buttons, and navigation elements.

Used for backgrounds, links, and accent colors.

Typography
